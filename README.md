# Bearing

A ten-minute career direction finder for Australians. Answer a short questionnaire and Bearing suggests a direction and ranks 204 jobs. It runs entirely on your phone. Nothing you answer is sent anywhere.

## Get the Android app

**[Download Bearing.apk](https://github.com/PunkRockDrifter/Bearing/releases/download/apk-latest/Bearing.apk)**

1. Open the link on your Android phone.
2. Open the downloaded file and tap **Install**. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser or file manager.
3. Open **Bearing**.

Android may warn that the app is from outside the Play Store. That's expected for a test version.

## Questions about a friend

If a friend sends you a Bearing invite link, tap it and answer the questions about them. It takes about five minutes and needs no install.
