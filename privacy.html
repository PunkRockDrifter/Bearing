Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026.

The short version

Your answers and results are stored only on your own device, inside the app. There are no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. The only time anything is sent to us is if you choose to send feedback.

What is stored, and where

Your answers, results, settings, any friends' views you add and, if you choose to enter it, your own email address for invites are saved in the app's storage on this device. We can't see them. "Delete all my data" in Settings, or clearing the app's data in your phone's settings, removes them.

When information leaves your device

Only when you choose to share it. Invite links, friend reply links, results links and codes contain answers in encoded form. Anyone you send them to can read those answers, so share them only with people you trust. They pass through whatever app you use to send them (for example a messaging or email app), under that app's own privacy terms.

Asking a friend

When you send an invite, the link contains your first name and, if you typed one, your email address, so your friend's answers can be sent back to you. Your friend sees these details, can't change them, and their email app sends the reply from their own account. The reply contains only their answers about you and their first name, and never your email address. We don't send, receive or store these messages.

Feedback

The results page may ask "Did this hit the mark?". Answering is optional, and nothing is sent unless you press Send. If you do, we receive:

  • your feedback ratings, the jobs you ticked, and your comment if you write one;
  • your age range and state, only if you choose to add them;
  • only if you tick "Include my anonymous answers": your answers as codes (which options you picked and your ratings), your computed results, and your top-matched jobs.

We never receive your name, your email address, anything you wrote in your own words, friends' names or comments, or past job titles. The app shows exactly what will be sent before you send it.

Feedback is stored in a Google Sheet that only we can access, and is used only to improve Bearing's questions, scoring and job list. It isn't sold or shared. We don't keep your IP address. Each submission has a random ID shown to you and saved in Settings; quote it to us to have that submission deleted.

AI coach

The AI coach is off by default. When turned on, it copies a prompt containing your results to your clipboard. Nothing is sent by Bearing. If you paste it into an AI service, that service's privacy terms apply.

Children

Bearing is intended for people aged 16 and over.
